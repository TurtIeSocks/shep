The house style

Cute on the
outside. Exact
where it counts.

shep is a process manager written in Rust. The sheep are a mnemonic, not a costume — so the personality lives in prose, illustration and color, and drops out the moment a misread would cost someone a process.

Three rules
  1. Whimsy in prose, plain in reference. The homepage says flock. The config table says process.
  2. Never charming about damage. kill, delete, errors and exit codes stay literal.
  3. Every themed word has a straight twin. bleats is also logs. Forever.
01

The marks

A sheep

One managed process. Singular only — a set of them is the flock, never "sheeps" and never a bare plural.

A dog

A plugin the shepherd supervises for its own sake: metrics, bark. Dogs work for the shepherd. A dog is never the daemon.

shep.
The wordmark

Lowercase always, even at the start of a sentence — it is the binary's name. Bricolage Grotesque 800, tracking −5%.

Do
  • Keep 4px of clear fleece around the mark's outline at every size.
  • Below 32px wide, drop the legs and use the head-and-fleece silhouette only.
  • Put the mark on meadow, butter or ink. Never on grass — the fleece stops reading.
Don't
  • Add a second animal. One sheep, one dog, that's the cast.
  • Rotate, gradient-fill, or add a drop shadow inside the mark.
  • Draw the sheep in distress to illustrate an error. Errors get a color, not a face.
